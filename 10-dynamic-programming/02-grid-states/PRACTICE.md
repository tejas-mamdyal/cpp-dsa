# Independent practice

Use the corresponding lesson bounds. For each task provide the full input/output contract,a simple baseline,state definition,trace,proof,costs and normal/boundary/counterexample checks. Attempt without copying worked source.

## 1. Exercise 1

Count monotone routes on grids with zero rows,one row and one cell.

## 2. Exercise 2

Place obstacles at origin,destination and an interior bottleneck.

## 3. Exercise 3

Compare one-row obstacle compression to independent route enumeration on all3x3 masks.

## 4. Exercise 4

Minimize signed cell sums and explain why negative values do not create cycles.

## 5. Exercise 5

Reconstruct a minimum grid route and verify adjacency,cost and endpoints.

## 6. Exercise 6

Compare triangle bottom-up minima to enumerating endpoint choices.

## 7. Exercise 7

Find a minimum falling path while preserving the previous row during updates.

## 8. Exercise 8

Return largest all-one square coordinates and area from its side state.

## 9. Exercise 9

Count every all-one square and compare to direct rectangle scans.

## 10. Exercise 10

Return both collectors routes without double-counting shared cells.

## 11. Exercise 11

Reconstruct a no-repeated-task schedule with negative rewards.

## 12. Exercise 12

Count signed grid paths whose sum is divisible by k and compare to enumeration.
