# Phase 9 final assessment

Allow150 minutes. Close worked sources and hints. Choose one independent exercise from each unit,including at least one reconstruction task and one assumption/counterexample task. Complete a project extension when this phase requires a project. Announce chosen tasks before opening any code.

Score100 points:correct independent implementations40,contracts and boundary behavior15,state/correctness reasoning25,time/space analysis10,meaningful direct-reference tests10. Pass at80 with at least half the correctness and reasoning points on every submitted implementation. Hardcoded examples earn no implementation points.

For each task,define its state or invariant in one sentence,derive transitions or exclusions,trace a tiny case,and explain why the method terminates. Test empty/minimum,duplicate,unreachable or impossible cases according to its domain. Change one constraint and say whether the method still applies. Record hints used and retry those tasks without copying a week later.

Instructor/peer selection should mix units without revealing pattern names. Use the separate unit hints only after submission and self-scoring. General DSA mastery is established by transfer and explanation,not only running supplied programs.
