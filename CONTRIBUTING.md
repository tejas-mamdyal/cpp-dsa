# Contributing

Keep additions small and educational. Put them after the concepts they need;update the
chapter README when adding a lesson. Prefer clear names and ordinary control flow.

Explain the idea,its assumptions and the important boundary. Avoid repeating audit text
in every source. Include a normal case and a boundary case. For an algorithm,explain why
it works and its cost. Preserve the distinction between a result value and its witness.

Compile only the changed source with C++17 and warnings enabled,then run its meaningful
cases. Comment-only edits do not require rebuilding the whole course. Check relative
links. Keep binaries and local editor settings out of commits.

License selection is left to the owner.
