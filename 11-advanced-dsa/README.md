## Core and specialist routes

Placement core: coordinate compression,Fenwick sums/inversions,segment point queries,
lazy propagation,sparse RMQ;prefix/KMP/Z,hash verification;Euler intervals and binary
lifting;SCC,bridges,articulation;gcd,sieve,factors,modular power and combinations;
orientation,segment intersection and convex hull. Read units numerically the first time.

Specialist challenges after the core:Mo ordering,rollback and persistent versions,
suffix arrays/automata,Aho-Corasick,heavy-light decomposition,flow/min-cut,2-SAT,
CRT,calipers and geometric divide-and-conquer. These are explained locally;they are
not prerequisites for an initial general placement mock. Return to their earlier
numbered lessons before attempting a later specialist source. Optional further reading
is in [references](REFERENCES.md);the teaching and executable examples are local.
