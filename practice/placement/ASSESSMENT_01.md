# Cumulative assessment 01

Allow210 minutes. Closed notes;choose any three implementation rounds among the five below before starting,then complete the foundations/build round and discuss the remaining two verbally. Record the chosen rounds so scoring cannot cherry-pick after seeing outcomes.

## Foundations/build round

Write a complete console program reading n followed by n signed integers,printing count,sum and average. Reject malformed input and explain integer versus floating division. Trace variable updates and overflow bounds.

## Mixed round P01

Given integers and a target,return two distinct original indices whose values sum to target,or absence. Duplicate values at different positions are legal.

## Mixed round P13

Group words that have identical character multiplicities. Keep every occurrence;state a deterministic group-output convention.

## Mixed round P25

Return a farthest reachable vertex and its hop count from a source in an undirected graph;report unreachable vertices separately.

## Mixed round P37

Return original indices of one longest strictly increasing subsequence. Check both increasing indices and values,including duplicate values.

## Mixed round P49

Return the largest rectangle under a histogram of nonnegative heights. Include equal heights and an empty histogram.

## Assessment scoring

Foundations/build25 points;three implementations15 each;two verbal derivations5 each;cross-topic correctness and complexity10;tests and communication10. Pass80 and at least half in every implementation and foundations round. Check full outputs with independently derived tiny references. Record any hints and redo the weak units before the next assessment.
