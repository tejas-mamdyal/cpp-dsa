# Shared interview rubric

Score100:implementation and complete result40;contract/boundaries15;correctness reasoning20;time/space10;tests10;communication5. Pass80 with no unsolved task and at least half implementation/reasoning credit per task. Supplied examples alone earn no correctness proof. Record hints;retry hinted tasks independently after a week.

Before coding:restate the input,output,limits,ties and failure policy. During coding:name the invariant and test one tiny case. After coding:walk through a boundary,an adversarial case and changed constraints. A peer should conceal topic titles and inspect the complete witness rather than only its length.
