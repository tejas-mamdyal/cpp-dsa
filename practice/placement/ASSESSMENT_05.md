# Cumulative assessment 05

Allow210 minutes. Closed notes;choose any three implementation rounds among the five below before starting,then complete the foundations/build round and discuss the remaining two verbally. Record the chosen rounds so scoring cannot cherry-pick after seeing outcomes.

## Foundations/build round

Explain a recursion tree,its base cases and stack depth;rewrite one recursive traversal iteratively. Prove which order properties the replacement preserves.

## Mixed round P05

Given strictly positive values and a positive target,return the shortest nonempty subarray length with sum at least target,or0.

## Mixed round P17

Decide whether a byte string can become a palindrome after deleting at most one character.

## Mixed round P29

Decide whether every component of an undirected graph admits a two-coloring. Include self-loops and isolated vertices.

## Mixed round P41

Return the area of the largest all-one rectangle in a rectangular binary matrix,including empty input.

## Mixed round P53

Return the k most frequent distinct words,breaking frequency ties alphabetically. k is valid.
