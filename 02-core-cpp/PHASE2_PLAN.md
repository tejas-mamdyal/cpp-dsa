# Phase 2: core C++ and standard-library fluency

Prerequisite: Phase1's eight units and checkpoints. Phase2 has two chapter targets:
at least100 reviewed core-C++ programs and60 reviewed STL programs, plus two projects.
Migrated examples are source material, not automatically reviewed/completed lessons.

## Completed delivery

The original minimum was100 core programs and60 STL programs. Actual delivery is
122 core programs and66 STL programs plus two projects. The expanded operator,
parsing and standard-question work does not reduce the other units' coverage.

| Unit | Delivered coverage |
|---|---|
|Arrays/matrices|16 introductory programs plus multiplication, rotation and spiral questions|
|Strings|12 introductions/applications including counts, palindrome, anagrams, runs and substring search|
|Functions/references/pointers|Defaults, constexpr/static locals, aliases, const pointer forms, bounds, lifetimes and function pointers|
|Vectors|12 introductions/applications including capacity, invalidation, second distinct maximum, zero movement, merge, rotate and deduplication|
|Parsing|13 independent whitespace, EOF, count, delimiter, record, matrix, quoted and sentinel examples|
|Classes|16 record/class programs including construction, invariants, composition, copying, enums and operator/member-pointer syntax|
|Polymorphism|8 interface/dispatch programs; allocation is taught before the deletion bridge|
|Ownership|12 dynamic-storage/RAII programs including move, weak observation and safe limited casts|
|Errors/files|12 programs including optional absence, recovery, noexcept, validation, persistence and formatting|
|Language bridges|Types/qualifiers, aliases, auto/range loops and precise input transitions|
|STL|66 programs across sequences/iterators, adaptors/ordered, hashing, lambdas/comparators and algorithm/numeric interfaces|
|Projects|Persistent contact manager and encapsulated student-record manager|

Every teaching unit has notes, exercises, hints and a scored checkpoint. The
[course entry](README.md) and exact route
identify bridge lessons whose dependencies cross directory boundaries.

## Completion and validation

Recursion implementations, asymptotic proofs, linked structures and full DSA
patterns remain Phase3 onward. Templates/metaprogramming, concurrency, custom
allocators and coroutine-frame engineering belong to the later C++ engineering
phase. Completion here means the agreed beginner/core/STL curriculum, not every
possible C++ standard-library facility or interview question.
