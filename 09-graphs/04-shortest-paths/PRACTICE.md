# Independent practice

Use the corresponding lesson bounds. For each task provide the full input/output contract,a simple baseline,state definition,trace,proof,costs and normal/boundary/counterexample checks. Attempt without copying worked source.

## 1. Exercise 1

Reconstruct an unweighted shortest route including source=target and unreachable target.

## 2. Exercise 2

Compare multi-source BFS to the minimum of separate-source distances.

## 3. Exercise 3

Compare Dijkstra to Bellman-Ford on small nonnegative graphs with zero cycles.

## 4. Exercise 4

Compare0-1 BFS to Dijkstra on binary-weight graphs.

## 5. Exercise 5

Construct a negative-edge failure of settled-vertex Dijkstra.

## 6. Exercise 6

Distinguish a negative cycle anywhere,one reachable from source,and one affecting target.

## 7. Exercise 7

Compare Bellman-Ford to all-pairs distances when no reachable negative cycle exists.

## 8. Exercise 8

Reconstruct a Floyd route or reject it when a reachable negative cycle can reach the destination.

## 9. Exercise 9

Protect Floyd arithmetic on dense negative-cycle graphs.

## 10. Exercise 10

Find the minimum maximum terrain jump rather than the minimum sum of jumps.

## 11. Exercise 11

Reconstruct a best route using at most K edges and verify its edge count.

## 12. Exercise 12

Extend the campus planner with an independently verified via-building query.
