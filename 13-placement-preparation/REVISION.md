# Revision and weak-area drills

Use the explicit repository learning route. First verify fundamentals (types,operators,
loops,functions),then ownership/parsing/STL,complexity and recursion,linear structures,
search/window/greedy patterns,trees,graphs,DP,and the selected advanced route.

For each unit:explain one invariant without code,trace one boundary,implement one problem
from a blank file,and compare against a tiny direct reference. Below80 on the shared rubric:
return to that unit's notes and practice,then try a changed problem after a week.
Do not compensate for a weak prerequisite by memorizing advanced solutions.

Weeks1-2:array/window and string sets;weeks3-4:trees/graphs;weeks5-6:DP and design;weeks7-8:
mixed timed rounds and project defense. This is an example revision schedule after studying
the full course,not a promise to learn the entire curriculum in eight weeks. Use the six
cumulative assessments at suitable milestones and preserve feedback in the notebook.
