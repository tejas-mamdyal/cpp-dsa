# Independent practice

Use C++17 and the corresponding worked lesson domains, including small exponential bounds. For each task provide a contract, baseline, hand trace, proof, complexity and meaningful normal/boundary/counterexample checks. Attempt independently before opening hints.

## 1. Task 1

Return every palindrome partition ofabba and independently verify pieces and concatenation.

## 2. Task 2

Compare minimum cuts against cut masks on binary strings through length 6.

## 3. Task 3

Restore 0010 and 1111 as IPv 4 addresses; reject too-short input.

## 4. Task 4

Segmentaaaa usinga andaa; also test an impossibleb.

## 5. Task 5

Return every simple route on a 3x 3 maze with center blocked.

## 6. Task 6

Explain why a one-cellA board cannot matchAA.

## 7. Task 7

Search a dictionary with duplicate entries and an empty word.

## 8. Task 8

Compare gold collection with a separate visited-array baseline on 2x 3 grids.

## 9. Task 9

Count down/right routes on a 3x 4 grid before and after adding an obstacle.

## 10. Task 10

Return index-group witnesses for positive equal-sum bucket partitioning.

## 11. Task 11

Count target 0 subsets of six zeros with meet in the middle.

## 12. Task 12

Compare closest subset difference with all masks for signed inputs through length 8.
