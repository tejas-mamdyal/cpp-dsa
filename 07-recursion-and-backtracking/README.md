Complete for the defined Phase 5 scope: 130 worked programs (50 backtracking and 80 trees/heaps), two projects, 108 unit exercises and 18 mixed exercises. Complete Phases 1-4 first.

Read notes, then numbered programs, then practice without copying. Every source is standalone C++17 with comments covering contracts, state, hand traces, correctness, costs and pitfalls. Keep assertions enabled. Notes identify core, reinforcement and challenge routes; first reading still follows numerical order so concepts precede variants.
